## Changes

- Main menu now contains Settings, Current channel management, Audio-only listening, Help & feedback, and Exit. Removed playlist import, Automatic sources, the duplicate global-caption entry, and the duplicate programme-guide entry.
- Renamed the settings entry and dialog to Settings.
- Grouped existing settings under Picture & playback, Captions & translation, Interface & display, and Help & about, with section headings and Material dividers.
- Retained diagnostics as a switch under Help & about, alongside feedback and version information.
- Current channel management retains favorite/unfavorite, source selection/enable/disable, and playback. Removed add-source, official-watch-link, and diagnostics entries.
- An empty source-management list now displays a short empty state rather than prompting the viewer to configure a stream.

## Preserved behavior

Automatic source refresh, source ranking, playback recovery, channel identity/data, favorites, programme-guide access outside the main menu, caption consent/save/cancel logic, settings outside-touch dismissal policy, theme tokens, and the landscape-lock control are unchanged. Landscape hardware remains explicitly optional.

The old internal playlist/import helper implementations were not deleted; their user-facing entry points are removed. This is a focused navigation change, not a source-engine rewrite.

## Artifacts and signing

The sideload APK and Play-upload AAB use the existing private upload certificate. Its key material is never included in distribution artifacts or the public repository. Google Play App Signing may use a different app signing certificate; a Play-installed app must be updated through Play if a sideload APK's certificate differs. Do not uninstall solely to force an update without backing up settings.

## Validation boundary

APK / AAB builds completed, 102 JVM tests passed, lint reported 0 errors and 114 warnings, APK and AAB signatures verified, and 16 KiB ZIP alignment passed. Catalog/source/guide/logo assets and startup-video content were compared with 0.8.25 and preserved. Detailed results are recorded in the versioned validation manifest. Actual TV/phone/tablet interaction, focus, scroll behavior, and Play Console processing must still be checked on devices and in Console. No physical-device UI test is claimed.

## Suggested device checks

1. Open the main menu by Back and confirm the simplified list; Back and outside-touch still close navigation overlays.
2. Open Settings from either the main menu or playback toolbar. Navigate through every section using D-pad and touch. Only interactive controls should receive ordinary D-pad focus.
3. Confirm 4K, frame-rate matching, aspect ratio and status-bar choices retain their values and handlers.
4. Open captions from Settings; unchanged settings must keep Save disabled, translation must remain disabled when captions are off, and consent validation/save/cancel must still work.
5. Toggle diagnostics on and off under Help & about. Open feedback and return to playback.
6. Open current channel management and verify each remaining action performs its labeled operation. An empty source list must not open an add-source form.
7. Verify existing guide, favorites, playback, startup refresh and network-change refresh on authorized test streams.
